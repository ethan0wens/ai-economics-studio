# Limitations and independent review

Use the studio to explain a planning model, its inputs and the cost evidence that applies. This register distinguishes an implemented estimate, a modelling assumption and evidence that requires independent review.

## Scope and evidence register

| Area | Implemented scope | Reviewer action or remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Products and geography | Microsoft-first configuration catalogue with explicit families, billing regions and UK commercial reviews. Custom manual plans can represent other known assumptions. | Check exact support and regional evidence. Unsupported or missing scope remains visible. There is no automatic rate conversion or global catalogue completeness claim. |
| Foundry capabilities | Dated exact model/version, mode, region, modalities, features and selected integration evidence. | Confirm tenant quota, capacity, deployment availability, performance and lifecycle. Private networking and security topology are outside the logical diagram. |
| Copilot Credits | Declared credit quantities, declared eligibility window, one recognised UK pooled tariff and separate supported prompt path. | Confirm current meter conversions, allowance eligibility, negotiated capacity, subscription terms and actual consumption. Plan-specific or speculative rights stay unresolved. |
| Commercial licensing | Exact reviewed public offers and evidence for some prerequisite, bundle and same-group paths. Affects declared seats and illustrative replacement amounts. | Confirm actual named-user assignments, contract inclusions, purchase/cancellation timing and customer eligibility. Foreign or negotiated terms need separate evidence. |
| Charges and financial reporting | Steady monthly usage, rounded line items and an annualised estimate. Price forecasting varies a hypothetical price factor. | Tax, contractual currency conversion, cash-flow schedules, usage growth, billing proration, blended future workload and financial ROI need separate evaluation. |
| Adoption and potential value | Optional accepted value assumptions and temporary, explicit single-workload comparisons. | Independently confirm adoption, task data, achievable time and declared cashable savings. Confirm missing implementation and operating expenses separately. |
| Provisioned capacity | Hourly tariffs, customer quote allocations, weighting and a PAYG comparison for a declared workload. | Validate throughput, latency, capacity and actual quote terms. Reservation term, allocation and hours are modelled declarations. |
| Evidence availability | Scheduled bounded retail and evidence checks with provenance, expiry and rollback guard. | Source changes or unreviewed selectors can leave information unresolved. Review snapshot date and health. Provider availability, review timing and publication are operational dependencies. |
| Runtime and files | In-browser processing with explicit JSON import/export and locally rendered diagrams and PDF. | Manage copies securely. JSON has a size/depth limit and a literal schema version. Large diagrams, unusual glyphs and device/browser constraints can affect PDF presentation. |
| Security and governance | Same-origin delivery, CSP, scoped production secrets, and advisory findings from declarations. Environment Review describes the declared plan and dated evidence. | Confirm provider infrastructure policies and customer controls. This documentation contains no independent penetration test, legal assurance or compliance certification. |

See [official sources and review scope](05-catalogue-contract.md#exact-price-identity), [catalogue coverage](05-catalogue-contract.md#coverage-and-freshness) and [technical tests](07-verification-and-tests.md).

## Assumptions and future considerations

A financial reviewer should record the exact editable environment, applied catalogue and assessment date. Share assumptions and unresolved charges with a report. A developer should distinguish future possibilities such as authenticated tenant read APIs, workflow simulation, scenario calendars or alternative provider models from the current release. Such changes would require new product and architectural decisions, data protection review, evidence and tests. The implemented architecture remains browser planning on static assets.
