The checked-in bootstrap manifest has no prices. Production refreshes replace it only after validating the live feed. Direct Microsoft requests from the development workspace are blocked by its network policy. GitHub Actions reached the official feed and confirmed the exact meter identity. Mock responses exercise refresh mechanics and are kept outside production assets. Service references describe the billing context. They do not establish a specific SKU's availability, entitlement or customer price.

When refreshing, the exact selector and source effective date are carried into the catalogue. An identity, tier, currency or unit change, missing meter, ambiguous current result, or movement over 50% stops publication for review. New scopes stay unknown until explicitly supported or manually priced.
