## Corrections and current formulas

Explicitly owned expenditure is now separate from the incremental project ledger, including its retail comparator. Ambiguous duplicate SKU purchases and multiple eligible prepaid pools become unresolved. A usage profile referenced by several agents requires separate demand before multiplying it. Shared deployment costs remain one charge. Pack allocation follows inventory order so duplicate workspaces reconcile even when agent-specific rates differ.

Token quantity equals executions × model calls × (1 + retry fraction) × the corresponding tokens per call. Flat lines divide the declared base units by the published unit divisor before applying the rate. Graduated lines apply only the portion in each band. An object-level agreement takes precedence over an environment agreement; an explicit retail choice can bypass the wider agreement. A specific forecast replaces the global forecast, with fixed prices forecast only on explicit opt-in. Amounts round half up once per ledger line. Annual known run-rate is the sum of rounded monthly amounts × 12. Tax, foreign exchange, retrospective billing reconciliation and invoice timing are outside the estimate.
